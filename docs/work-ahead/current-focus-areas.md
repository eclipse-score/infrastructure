# Current Focus Areas

This page summarizes high-value work themes that are currently relevant across the infrastructure.

It is intentionally concise and should be updated as focus changes.

## Focus Themes

### Workflow reuse and CI consistency

- why now: reusable workflows and shared validation behavior are central to multi-repository consistency
- where to help: [CI/CD workflows](../guides/cicd-workflows.md)
- related WBS: [CI/CD Platform work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-cicd)

### Bazel and dependency clarity

- why now: build and dependency clarity affects reproducibility, CI behavior, and artifact quality
- where to help: [Bazel and dependencies](../guides/bazel-and-dependencies.md)
- related WBS: [Build Platform work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-build)

### Artifact publication baseline

- why now: stable publication and distribution paths are needed for reliable reuse
- where to help: [Artifact publishing](../guides/artifact-publishing.md)
- related WBS: [Artifact Platform work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-artifacts)

### Test reporting quality

- why now: contributors need fast and clear feedback from CI test execution
- where to help: [Testing and reporting](../guides/testing-and-reporting.md)
- related WBS: [Testing Platform work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-testing)

### Compliance workflow integration

- why now: license, vulnerability, and SBOM processes need predictable integration with build and CI workflows
- where to help: [License compliance and SBOM](../guides/license-compliance-and-sbom.md)
- related WBS: [Security & Compliance work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-security)

### Documentation as operational guidance

- why now: contributors need reliable task guidance, not just conceptual descriptions
- where to help: [Documentation publishing](../guides/documentation-publishing.md)
- related WBS: [Documentation Platform work packages](work-breakdown-structure.md#wbs-documentation)
